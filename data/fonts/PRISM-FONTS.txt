Static instances generated with fontTools 4.61.1, no subsetting:

                        inplace=True, updateFontNames=True).save(destination)

All 66 Russian Cyrillic letters, including capital/lowercase Yo, are present
in all three bundled faces. The renderer retains DDNet's existing fallback
chain for other missing glyphs. Default DDNet text remains DejaVu Sans.

Prism headings apply X scale to glyph quad width, horizontal bearing,
advance, kerning and ellipsis metrics. Vertical dimensions and glyph UVs
are unchanged. This is actual glyph stretching, not tracking. UI callers
restore scale 1.0 and the default font after rendering the Prism overlay.
